# Pantry — Chapter 18: Forecasting

## Cold-open candidates

- **Profitable but broke** — the cash-crunch puzzle. Used as Ch 18 hook.
- **COVID 2020 forecast failures** — illustrates tail-event risk.
- **Big 5 Sporting Goods 2020 → 2021** — mean reversion problem.

## Specifications

- **"Pro forma"** — forward-looking financial statements.
- **"Variable"** vs. **"fixed"** costs — scaling vs. constant.
- **"Operating leverage"** — fixed costs amplify sales-driven income changes.
- **"Plug variable"** — cash, the balance-sheet residual.

## Mechanism explanations (reusable)

- **The sales-drives-everything chain** — sales → variable costs → income statement → A/R, inventory → balance sheet.
- **Why growth eats cash** — working capital + capex front-loaded.
- **Operating leverage** — fixed costs amplify cyclical income variation.

## Worked-example structures

- **Clear Lake monthly pro forma** — full 12-month I/S, B/S, CF.
- **Recession scenario** — 40% sales drop → 65% income drop.
- **Sensitivity matrix** — WACC × terminal growth → fair value range.

## Quotable phrasings

- "Profit isn't cash."
- "Growing profits and growing cash are not the same thing."
- "A single point estimate is overconfident."
- "Cash flexibility is asymmetric protection."

## Counter-arguments and limits

- **Mean reversion** — high growth rarely persists indefinitely.
- **Tail events** — COVID 2020, 2022 inflation. Forecasts based on normal conditions fail.
- **AI-productivity uncertainty** — current structural unknown.
