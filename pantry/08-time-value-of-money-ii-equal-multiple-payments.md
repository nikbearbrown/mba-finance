## Specifications

- **"Annuity"** — equal payments over finite term. Ordinary (end of period) vs. due (beginning).
- **"Perpetuity"** — equal payments forever. Constant or growing.
- **"APR"** vs. **"EAR"** — stated vs. effective. Always convert before comparing.

## Mechanism explanations (reusable)

- **Why perpetuities have finite PV** — geometric series convergence.
- **The (1+r) bridge from ordinary to due** — one period of timing earlier.
- **Why early loan payments are interest-heavy** — interest on the highest balance.
- **Why effective ≠ stated** — compounding within the year.

## Quotable phrasings

- "The right discount rate is, as always, what you would actually earn on the money."
- "A dollar ten years away is worth substantially less than a dollar today."
- "The arithmetic is built into the annuity formula."

## Counter-arguments and limits

- **Sequence-of-returns risk** — arithmetic alone doesn't capture market timing risk in retirement portfolios. Useful for Ch 15 nuance.
- **Real annuity products** have features (inflation adjustments, survivor benefits) the formula ignores.
