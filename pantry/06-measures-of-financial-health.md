## Specifications

- **"Healthy"** firm — passes liquidity, solvency, profitability checks against industry norms.
- **"Leverage"** — multiple meanings: equity multiplier, debt-to-equity, debt-to-assets. Specify which.
- **"Profitable"** — depends on the denominator. Profit margin, ROA, ROE all measure different profitabilities.
- **"Trailing"** vs. **"forward"** P/E — different inputs, different uses.

## Mechanism explanations (reusable)

- **Why ratios beat raw numbers.** Scale-control, relationships across statements, benchmarking enabled.
- **DuPont identity** — multiplicative decomposition, three drivers visible.
- **TIE covenants** — bond-market enforcement of solvency.
- **Buybacks → EPS arithmetic** — share-count reduction as EPS lever; not always shareholder value.

- **Quantitative ML approaches** vs. ratio-based fundamental analysis.
- **Screens vs. diagnosis** — same ratios, different rules. Open question; chapter punts.
