## Specifications

- **"Payback"** — how fast you get your money back.
- **"NPV"** — value creation in today's dollars.
- **"IRR"** — implied annual return; flawed in specific cases.
- **"MIRR"** — IRR with realistic reinvestment.
- **"PI"** — value per dollar invested; right tool for capital rationing.

## Mechanism explanations (reusable)

- **Why NPV beats IRR on mutually exclusive projects** — scale matters for value, not for rates.
- **Why multiple IRRs can occur** — sign changes in cash flows.
- **Why PI ranks correctly under budget constraints** — value per dollar invested.
- **Replacement chain vs. EAA** — handling unequal-life comparisons.

## Quotable phrasings

- "When NPV and IRR disagree, NPV wins."
- "Bad NPV decisions usually result from bad cash flow estimation, not from bad arithmetic."
- "Real options have real value but are hard to quantify."
- "Strategic considerations should be specifically articulated, not used as catch-all justification."

## Counter-arguments and limits

- **WACC-for-everything bias** — low-risk projects under-approved, high-risk over-approved.
- **Real options understated** — standard NPV misses flexibility value.
- **Strategic value** is real but hard to quantify.
- **Sunk costs** common error in cash flow estimation.
