# Pantry — Chapter 20: Risk Management

## Specifications

- **"Risk"** — uncertainty in either direction; not just downside.
- **"Hedging"** vs. **"speculation"** — same tools, opposite positions.
- **"Forward"** vs. **"future"** — customized vs. standardized.
- **"Option"** — asymmetric payoff (premium for protection).
- **"Swap"** — exchange of cash flow streams.

## Mechanism explanations (reusable)

- **Why hedging reduces firm value's volatility** — lower discount rate, higher PV.
- **Why credit-rating arbitrage drives swaps** — fixed-vs-floating spreads differ by credit quality.
- **Why VaR fails in tails** — calibrated on stable data, ignores regime changes.

## Worked-example structures

- **AA jet fuel** — concrete commodity-price exposure.
- **€10M payable hedged via forward, futures, or option** — comparison of three approaches.
- **Interest rate swap (Alpha vs. Beta)** — credit-spread arbitrage.

## Quotable phrasings

- "Hedging trades expected return for reduced variance."
- "The risks that destroy firms are typically the ones nobody saw coming."
- "Risk management is the discipline of acknowledging the threats explicitly."
- "Build enough financial cushion to survive shocks the model didn't predict."

## Counter-arguments and limits

- **Black Swans** — risks not in the register.
- **VaR limitations** — fat tails, regime changes.
- **Hedging cost** — premiums and bid-ask spreads add up.
- **Overhedging** — can be as bad as underhedging.
