## Specifications

- **"WACC"** — weighted average cost of capital. Hurdle rate.
- **"Cost of debt"** — YTM × (1 − T). Tax-adjusted.
- **"Cost of equity"** — multiple methods (CAPM, DGM); often disagree.
- **"Optimal capital structure"** — point where firm value maximized.

## Mechanism explanations (reusable)

- **Tax shield mechanics** — interest tax-deductible; equity dividends not.
- **MM Proposition I** — capital structure irrelevant in perfect markets.
- **MM Proposition II** — cost of equity rises with leverage to offset cheaper debt.
- **Trade-off** — tax shield benefit + distress cost penalty = optimum.

## Quotable phrasings

- "Debt is cheaper but increases default risk; equity is more expensive but more flexible."
- "WACC is the firm's universal hurdle rate."
- "The discount rate is the most sensitive input in the entire analysis."
- "Apple borrows because debt is cheaper than equity."

## Counter-arguments and limits

- **CAPM vs. DGM** disagreement on cost of equity.
- **Beta source variation** — Yahoo, Bloomberg, MarketWatch can differ by 0.3+.
- **Pecking order vs. trade-off** — both partially explain financing patterns.
