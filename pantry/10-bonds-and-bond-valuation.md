## Specifications

- **"Bond"** — structured loan with explicit cash flow contract.
- **"Coupon rate"** vs. **"YTM"** — fixed vs. market-set.
- **"Premium"** / **"discount"** / **"par"** — price relationship driven by coupon-vs-YTM.
- **"Duration"** — price sensitivity to 1% rate change. Longer/lower-coupon = more duration.
- **"Yield curve"** — yield vs. maturity plot; normal, inverted, flat.

## Mechanism explanations (reusable)

- **The bond pricing formula** — PV of coupons (annuity) + PV of par (single payment).
- **Why bond prices move inverse to rates** — fixed coupons must reprice when market alternatives change.
- **Why the yield curve inversion matters** — market expectations of future rate cuts.
- **Credit spread = bond YTM − Treasury YTM** at same maturity. Reusable for Ch 17.

## Worked-example structures

- **3M bond pricing** — applied to real bond.
- **Premium / discount example** — discount bond (4% coupon, 5% YTM, 15 years) prices to $895.
- **YTM solving** — iterative or `=RATE()`. Reusable.

## Quotable phrasings

- "Coupon rate is fixed; YTM varies."
- "Bond prices move opposite interest rates, not with them."
- "Ratings are opinions, not guarantees."
- "Pricing a bond is just present value of a mixed cash flow stream — applied to a specific stream."

## Counter-arguments and limits

- **Rating agency 2008 failure** — mortgage-backed securities mass-defaulted at AAA.
- **Realized return ≠ YTM** — coupon reinvestment at different rates.
- **Floating-rate and convertible bonds** — chapter doesn't price these explicitly.
- **Duration formula not derived** — kept qualitative for length.
