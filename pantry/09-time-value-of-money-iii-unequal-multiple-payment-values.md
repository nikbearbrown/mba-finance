## Specifications

- **"NPV"** — sum of present-valued cash flows, including initial investment as a negative.
- **"IRR"** — discount rate at which NPV = 0. Foreshadowed; full treatment Ch 16.
- **"Real options"** — flexibility value not captured in standard NPV. Foreshadowed.

## Mechanism explanations (reusable)

- **The general PV formula** — $PV = \sum CF_t / (1+r)^t$.
- **NPV decision rule** — accept if > 0, reject if < 0.
- **NPV value additivity** — NPV of combined projects = sum of individual NPVs. Critical for portfolio analysis.
- **Sensitivity to discount rate** — small rate changes produce large NPV changes for long horizons.

## Quotable phrasings

- "NPV is denominated in today's dollars."
- "The math is exact; the inputs are estimates."
- "A 'valuation' expressed as a single number is usually overconfident."

## Counter-arguments and limits

- **Real options** — standard NPV understates flexibility-rich projects.
- **Discount rate sensitivity** — WACC is itself estimated; small differences propagate.
- **IRR multiple-roots problem** — when cash flows change sign more than once. Foreshadowed.
