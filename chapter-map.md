# Chapter Map — Principles of Finance with LLMs

*Lightweight map built from OpenStax structure. Used to ground running-project proposals. Each chapter to be read in full when the rewrite happens.*

---

## Arc

The book moves through four phases:

- **Phase 1: Foundations (Ch 1–6)** — what finance studies, who the firm is for, the macro context, accounting and statements, ratio analysis.
- **Phase 2: The arithmetic of cash over time (Ch 7–11)** — TVM in three forms, bond valuation, stock valuation.
- **Phase 3: Measuring uncertainty (Ch 12–14)** — historical market data, statistical analysis, regression.
- **Phase 4: Decisions under uncertainty (Ch 15–20)** — investing personally, investing as a firm, raising capital, forecasting, working capital, risk management.

Every chapter introduces tools the next chapter depends on. A running project that threads the book has to advance through all four phases.

## Per-chapter map

### Ch 1 — Introduction to Finance
- **Concepts:** Three areas (business finance, investments, markets/institutions). Risk-return trade-off. Three business-finance decisions (working capital, capital budgeting, capital structure).
- **New capability:** Identify what kind of finance question you're asking; locate it in the right region of a balance sheet.
- **Vocab:** Finance, risk, return, primary/secondary markets, broker, dealer, intermediary.
- **Dig-deeper candidates:** Knightian uncertainty; the Peloton case; the $10K-over-30-years numerical example.

### Ch 2 — Corporate Structure and Governance
- **Concepts:** Sole proprietorship vs. partnership vs. LLC vs. C-corp vs. S-corp. Agency problems. Stakeholders vs. shareholders. Boards, executives, fiduciary duty.
- **New capability:** Tell which legal form a firm should use; identify who the firm is *for*; spot agency conflicts.
- **Vocab:** Limited liability, fiduciary, agency cost, governance.
- **Dig-deeper candidates:** Stakeholder vs. shareholder primacy debate; ESG investing; recent corporate governance controversies.

### Ch 3 — Economic Foundations: Money and Rates
- **Concepts:** What money is, the Fed, interest rates, yield curves, inflation, monetary policy.
- **New capability:** Read a yield curve; relate Fed actions to changes in firm financing costs.
- **Vocab:** Federal funds rate, real vs. nominal rate, term structure, inflation.
- **Dig-deeper candidates:** Inverted yield curve as recession indicator; 2022–2023 Fed tightening cycle.

### Ch 4 — Accrual Accounting Process
- **Concepts:** Accrual vs. cash basis. Revenue recognition. Matching principle. Journal entries.
- **New capability:** Translate transactions into accounting entries; explain why net income ≠ cash flow.
- **Vocab:** Accrual, recognition, matching, double-entry.
- **Dig-deeper candidates:** Earnings management cases; revenue recognition reforms (ASC 606).

### Ch 5 — Financial Statements
- **Concepts:** Income statement, balance sheet, cash flow statement, statement of equity. Notes to financial statements. The 10-K.
- **New capability:** Read a public company's 10-K and find specific items.
- **Vocab:** GAAP, MD&A, segment reporting.
- **Dig-deeper candidates:** Comparing 10-Ks across competitors; non-GAAP earnings adjustments.

### Ch 6 — Measures of Financial Health
- **Concepts:** Liquidity ratios, leverage ratios, profitability ratios, efficiency ratios, market-value ratios.
- **New capability:** Compute and interpret a complete ratio analysis on a real firm.
- **Vocab:** Current ratio, debt-to-equity, ROA, ROE, P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA.
- **Dig-deeper candidates:** Industry benchmark variation; DuPont decomposition.

### Ch 7 — TVM I: Single Payment Value
- **Concepts:** Future value, present value, compounding, discounting.
- **New capability:** Move a single cash flow through time at a given rate.
- **Vocab:** FV, PV, compound interest, simple interest.
- **Dig-deeper candidates:** Effect of compounding frequency; Rule of 72.

### Ch 8 — TVM II: Equal Multiple Payments
- **Concepts:** Annuities, perpetuities, ordinary vs. due, growing annuities.
- **New capability:** Value any stream of equal payments.
- **Vocab:** Annuity, perpetuity, amortization.
- **Dig-deeper candidates:** Lottery lump sum vs. annuity; mortgage amortization tables.

### Ch 9 — TVM III: Unequal Multiple Payments
- **Concepts:** Mixed cash flow streams. NPV of arbitrary projects. IRR.
- **New capability:** Value any sequence of unequal cash flows; compute NPV and IRR.
- **Vocab:** NPV, IRR, payback, discounted payback.
- **Dig-deeper candidates:** Multiple IRRs; NPV vs. IRR conflicts in ranking.

### Ch 10 — Bonds and Bond Valuation
- **Concepts:** Coupon, yield to maturity, duration, convexity. Bond pricing as PV of cash flows. Risk classes (Treasury, IG corporate, high yield, muni).
- **New capability:** Price a bond from its cash flows and a discount rate; compute YTM.
- **Vocab:** Coupon, par, yield, spread, duration.
- **Dig-deeper candidates:** Why duration matters in 2022's bond crash; muni-bond tax math.

### Ch 11 — Stocks and Stock Valuation
- **Concepts:** Dividend discount model, Gordon growth, FCF model, multiples.
- **New capability:** Build a valuation model for a public company's stock.
- **Vocab:** DCF, terminal value, growth rate, required return.
- **Dig-deeper candidates:** Why DCF often disagrees with market price; multiples vs. intrinsic-value approaches.

### Ch 12 — Historical Performance of US Markets
- **Concepts:** Long-run returns by asset class. Bear markets and recoveries. Inflation-adjusted vs. nominal.
- **New capability:** Read historical return data; compute realized risk premia.
- **Vocab:** Equity premium, drawdown, real return.
- **Dig-deeper candidates:** Equity-premium puzzle; Japan since 1990 as counter-example.

### Ch 13 — Statistical Analysis in Finance
- **Concepts:** Mean, variance, standard deviation. Distributions of returns. Correlation. Sharpe ratio.
- **New capability:** Compute risk metrics on a real return series.
- **Vocab:** Standard deviation, correlation, Sharpe ratio, normal distribution.
- **Dig-deeper candidates:** Fat tails and 2008/2020; non-normality of stock returns.

### Ch 14 — Regression Analysis in Finance
- **Concepts:** OLS regression, beta, R², CAPM as a regression.
- **New capability:** Run a regression of a stock's returns on the market and interpret beta.
- **Vocab:** OLS, beta, alpha, R², residual.
- **Dig-deeper candidates:** Fama-French factors; the failure of CAPM in some periods.

### Ch 15 — How to Think about Investing
- **Concepts:** Asset allocation, diversification, rebalancing. Active vs. passive. Investment policy statement.
- **New capability:** Build a personal asset allocation; write an investment policy statement.
- **Vocab:** Asset allocation, rebalancing, IPS.
- **Dig-deeper candidates:** Glide paths and target-date funds; the active-vs-passive empirical debate.

### Ch 16 — How Companies Think about Investing
- **Concepts:** Capital budgeting decision criteria. NPV vs. IRR vs. payback. Real options. Sunk cost.
- **New capability:** Take a project's projected cash flows and decide whether the firm should invest.
- **Vocab:** NPV rule, hurdle rate, real options, sunk cost.
- **Dig-deeper candidates:** Real-options valuation; corporate hurdle-rate setting.

### Ch 17 — How Firms Raise Capital
- **Concepts:** Debt vs. equity financing. IPOs, SEOs, private placements. Costs of issuance. WACC.
- **New capability:** Compute a firm's WACC; explain a firm's capital structure choice.
- **Vocab:** WACC, leverage, dilution, underwriting.
- **Dig-deeper candidates:** Direct listings vs. IPOs; SPAC structure; recent capital-structure controversies.

### Ch 18 — Financial Forecasting
- **Concepts:** Pro forma statements. Sales forecasting. Sensitivity and scenario analysis. Plug variables.
- **New capability:** Build a 3-statement projection for a firm under multiple scenarios.
- **Vocab:** Pro forma, plug, sensitivity, scenario.
- **Dig-deeper candidates:** Forecasting failures (e.g., COVID-era guidance withdrawals); the limits of mean-reversion assumptions.

### Ch 19 — Trade Credit and Working Capital
- **Concepts:** Cash conversion cycle. Inventory management. Trade credit terms (2/10 net 30). Short-term financing.
- **New capability:** Compute a firm's cash conversion cycle; recommend working-capital changes.
- **Vocab:** Days sales outstanding, days inventory, cash conversion cycle.
- **Dig-deeper candidates:** Just-in-time inventory tradeoffs; supply-chain finance during COVID.

### Ch 20 — Risk Management and the Financial Manager
- **Concepts:** Identifying, measuring, hedging, transferring risk. Insurance, derivatives, diversification. Enterprise risk management.
- **New capability:** Construct a risk register for a firm; recommend hedging strategies.
- **Vocab:** Hedge, derivative, ERM, value at risk.
- **Dig-deeper candidates:** VaR limits and 2008; corporate hedging programs (airlines, miners).

## Implications for project design

Three observations from the map:

1. **The book front-loads accounting.** Chapters 4–6 are deeply accounting-flavored. Any project that doesn't engage with reading and computing from financial statements will feel hollow through a third of the book.

2. **The TVM block (7–9) needs concrete cash flows to work on.** A project that produces no cash flow stream by chapter 7 will have nothing to discount.

3. **The investing/capital-budgeting/forecasting block (15–18) is where the project really earns its keep.** Whatever the learner has built up to chapter 14 has to be useful for making real decisions in 15–20.

A good running project hits all three: produces cash flows the learner discounts, generates real numbers the learner runs ratios and regressions on, and culminates in decisions in the back third of the book.
