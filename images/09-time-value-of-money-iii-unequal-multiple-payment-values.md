# Image briefs — Chapter 9: TVM III — Mixed Streams and NPV

## Figure 9.1 — The $450K project: discount rates compared

**Where:** Hook section.

**Subject.** Bar chart showing NPV at discount rates 0%, 4%, 7% (breakeven), 10%, 15%. Bars cross from positive to negative around the 7% mark.

**Caption.** *Same project, same cash flows. The discount rate decides accept or reject.*

---

## Figure 9.2 — PV decomposition for a mixed stream

**Where:** Concept 1.

**Subject.** Bar chart with a different bar for each year of the $450K project. Each bar's height shows the nominal cash flow; an internal section shows the present value at 10%. Visualizes how distant cash flows shrink in present-value terms.

**Caption.** *Each year's cash flow loses value the further into the future it sits. Present value is what's left after the discount.*

---

## Figure 9.3 — NPV sensitivity to discount rate

**Where:** Concept 3.

**Subject.** Smooth curve showing NPV as a function of discount rate, for a representative project. Curve crosses zero at the IRR, decreasing monotonically.

**Caption.** *NPV vs. discount rate. The shape — declining as the rate rises — is universal. The crossover point is the IRR.*
