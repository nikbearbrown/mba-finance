# Image briefs — Chapter 18

## Figure 18.1 — The pro forma chain

**Where:** Concept 2.

**Subject.** Flow diagram: Sales → variable costs (%age of sales), fixed costs ($), → operating income → tax → net income → flow to retained earnings → flow to balance sheet. Separate stream: A/R ← sales (lagged); A/P ← COGS (lagged); inventory ← forward COGS. Final: cash plug.

**Caption.** *The pro forma chain. Sales drives every other line, directly or indirectly.*

---

## Figure 18.2 — Cash crunch in growth

**Where:** Concept 2.

**Subject.** Three-line chart over a year: net income (smooth, growing); cumulative cash flow (volatile, dipping mid-year); cumulative free cash flow (depressed by working capital growth).

**Caption.** *A growing firm with poor cash forecasting can be perfectly profitable on the income statement and out of cash in the bank.*

---

## Figure 18.3 — Sensitivity table

**Where:** Concept 3.

**Subject.** Heatmap with WACC (rows) × terminal growth (columns) → DCF fair value (cells). Range from $80 to $180 across reasonable inputs.

**Caption.** *Sensitivity is the analyst's honest answer. A point estimate is overconfident.*
