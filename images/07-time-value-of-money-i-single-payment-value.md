## Figure 7.3 — The four directions of TVM

**Where:** Concept 2.

**Subject.** A diagram with the master formula $FV = PV(1+r)^n$ in the center and four arrows radiating out, each labeled with the variable being solved for and a representative real-world question:
- → FV: "What will my retirement account be worth in 30 years?"
- → PV: "How much should I pay today for a future cash flow?"
- → r: "What return did this investment earn?"
- → n: "How long until I reach my goal?"

**Caption.** *One formula, four directions of use. All of equity valuation is some flavor of these four moves.*
