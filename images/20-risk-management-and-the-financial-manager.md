## Figure 20.1 — Hedging instrument decision tree

**Where:** Concept 2.

**Subject.** Decision tree starting from "What kind of exposure?" branching to: known specific date/amount → forward; flexible date/amount → futures; want to keep upside → option; want to swap rate type → swap.

**Caption.** *Choosing the hedge. Each instrument fits a specific kind of exposure.*

## Figure 20.2 — Option payoff diagrams

**Where:** Concept 2.

**Subject.** Two payoff diagrams:
- Call option buyer: profit zero below strike, rises linearly above strike, less premium paid upfront.
- Put option buyer: profit zero above strike, rises as price falls below strike, less premium.

**Caption.** *Asymmetric payoffs. Buying options gives you protection without sacrificing upside, in exchange for a premium.*
