# Image briefs — Chapter 19

## Figure 19.1 — Cash conversion cycle visualized

**Where:** Concept 1.

**Subject.** Timeline showing: Day 0 (purchase inventory) → Day X (sell to customer, A/R created) → Day Y (receive cash from customer). Below: Day 0 (purchase inventory) → Day Z (pay supplier). The CCC = Y − Z.

**Caption.** *The cash conversion cycle. Short cycle = supplier-financed business. Negative cycle = customer-financed business.*
