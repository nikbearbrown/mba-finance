# Image briefs — Chapter 8: TVM II — Annuities

## Figure 8.1 — Lottery puzzle solved

**Where:** Hook section.

**Subject.** A timeline showing 10 annual payments of $120,000, with each payment's PV computed at 9% next to it. Sum of PVs = $770,119. Lump sum option ($787,000) shown for comparison. Cross-marks the breakeven rate.

**Caption.** *Same total dollars, different present values. The discount rate decides.*

---

## Figure 8.2 — Compound annuity vs. linear contributions

**Where:** Concept 2, after the two-workers example.

**Subject.** Side-by-side bar charts showing total contributions vs. final balance for two workers (Worker A 30→60 contributing $30K total, Worker B 20→60 contributing $40K total).

**Caption.** *Worker B contributed 33% more and ended with 82% more. Time in the market is the lever.*

---

## Figure 8.3 — Loan amortization curve

**Where:** Concept 3, after the amortization section.

**Subject.** Stacked bar chart for a 30-year mortgage. Each bar is one year. Each bar split between interest portion (red) and principal portion (green). Year 1 is mostly red; year 30 is mostly green.

**Caption.** *Early payments are interest. Late payments are principal. The mortgage feels like it's getting easier; in dollar terms, it is.*

---

## Figure 8.4 — Stated vs. effective rate

**Where:** Concept 3, in the EAR section.

**Subject.** A table showing stated APR alongside effective rates at different compounding frequencies (annual, quarterly, monthly, daily). Examples: 6%, 12%, 18%, 24%.

**Caption.** *The gap between stated and effective rates grows with both the rate and the compounding frequency.*
