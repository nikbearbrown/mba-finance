# Principles of Finance: with LLMs
## Compact Tik TOC Architecture

**Book type:** MBA / finance textbook  
**Status:** Retrospective architecture for developed content  

## Core Thesis

This book teaches finance as disciplined reasoning about value, risk, time, markets, capital, and uncertainty, with LLMs used to explain, check, and communicate analysis.

## Primary Reader

MBA students, business undergraduates, founders, and professionals who need finance literacy.

## Learning Arc

1. **Foundations:** time value, risk/return, financial statements, markets, and valuation.
2. **Decision tools:** capital budgeting, cost of capital, portfolios, bonds, stocks, and derivatives.
3. **Applied finance:** corporate policy, behavioral finance, crises, ethics, and AI-supported analysis.

## Chapter Architecture

Each chapter should connect a finance formula to a decision and a risk of misinterpretation.

## Terminal Capability

The reader can analyze basic finance problems, interpret valuation and risk outputs, and communicate assumptions clearly.

## Adoption Risks

- LLM explanations can hide arithmetic mistakes.
- Financial examples date quickly.
- Students may confuse model output with investment advice.
