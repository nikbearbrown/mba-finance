## Overview

**Audience.** Sophomore or junior business undergraduates taking their first finance course. One semester of accounting and one semester of college algebra assumed. Statistics not assumed — built up from scratch in chapters 13–14. No coding background assumed.

**Scope.** Twenty-chapter introductory finance course covering: the finance function, accounting and financial statements, time value of money, bond and stock valuation, market history, statistical and regression analysis as applied to financial data, capital budgeting, capital structure, financial forecasting, working capital, and risk management. Plus Chapter 00 — Claude Basics — as onboarding.
